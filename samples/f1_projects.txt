4 AI projects that can get you hired in Formula 1.

1. AI Driver Coach: imagine racing in a sim and hearing a brake later into turn 3. The model analyzes your telemetry in real time and gives instant feedback on how to improve.
2. Race Data Dashboard: looking at speed, braking and throttle separately does not show how a car performs. Build a dashboard that analyzes speed, tire degradation and track evolution, and compares cars lap by lap.
3. F1 Knowledge Chatbot: the model queries real-time data from a database and answers statistical questions, such as who the five fastest drivers were and their lap times. A regular chatbot cannot do that.
4. Results Predictor: train the model on historical performance data. Analyze each driver's qualifying results and use them to predict the race outcome before it starts.

You can use the FastF1 Python library to build these projects.
